Old Glory DC 2027 Player Signing Tracker

By Alistair Kirsch-Poole
Jul 18, 2026 - 12:00pm

Note: This article will be updated as signings and departures are announced. Check back here for the latest! Last updated on September 2, 2026.

While the exact details of the 2027 MLR season are up in the air, news has already started coming out about players who will definitely not be returning to the team in 2027. Thus, it is time once again to get the good old Player Tracker up and running. In these early days, updates are likely to be sparse. Player re-signings aren't expected until mid-September, and official announcements are often delayed to achieve a more even spread of annoucements throughout the off-season.

One twist this off-season is that none of last season's coaching staff will be returning to the OGDC coaching box in 2027, with new coaches not yet announced. While key players, especially those who are US qualified, are likely to be retained regardless of the coach in charge, ever coach brings their own strategy to roster construction and we could see a more turnover than usual in the playing squad.

Note: players marked as "Rumored" have been reported by other news organizations as returning or leaving, but this status has not been confirmed by the team or the players themselves. Players marked as "Questionable" are not guaranteed to be leaving, but have circumstances that make it unlikely that they will return.
